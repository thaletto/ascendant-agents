# Judgement hierarchy

Use precedence, not numeric voting. A larger pile of lower-ranked indications does not overrule a higher-ranked indication.

## Evidence layers

Apply these layers in order:

1. **Natal promise — D1.** Establish whether and how strongly the chart supports the topic from its relevant house, lord, karakas, occupants, aspects, dignity, and condition.
2. **Relevant divisional chart.** Confirm, refine, or weaken the D1 promise in the topic-specific division. Treat a missing required division as an explicit confidence limit.
3. **Vimshottari Dasha.** Determine whether the Mahadasha and Antardasha activate the relevant natal factors. Dasha opens a broad timing window; it does not manufacture an outcome absent from the natal and divisional evidence.
4. **Transit.** Use transits to narrow or trigger an already-supported window. A transit cannot override the natal promise or an inactive Dasha context.
5. **Ashtakavarga.** Use SAV as supporting strength for houses or transit conditions, not as a standalone prediction.

Attach Yoga and Jaimini evidence to the layer from which it is derived. Treat a named Yoga, Chara Karaka, Arudha Pada, Upapada, Rashi Drishti, Karakamsha, or Argala result as corroborating or modifying evidence unless it directly supplies the topic's primary indicator. Do not count the same underlying placement again merely because several derived artifacts repeat it.

## Evidence roles

Within each layer, rank evidence as:

1. **Primary:** directly governs the topic or timing activation.
2. **Corroborating:** independently repeats the same direction.
3. **Modifying:** changes strength, quality, delay, ease, or expression.
4. **Background:** provides context without deciding the outcome.

Higher layers determine direction. Lower layers refine strength and timing. When equal-rank primary evidence conflicts and neither has clear precedence, preserve the result as **mixed** and reduce confidence.

## Synthesis test

Before concluding, check:

- every supporting insight still traces to a distinct calculation fact;
- every opposing insight appears in the reconciliation;
- repeated derivatives have been merged rather than treated as extra votes;
- the conclusion follows the highest-ranked decisive evidence;
- timing claims contain both a natal topic connection and an active Dasha connection before transit is used to narrow them.

For example, Saturn in the D1 seventh house can be modifying evidence for delay or seriousness in partnership. An active Saturn period strengthens a marriage window only when Saturn genuinely activates the relationship factors and the D1 and D9 support the event. State the supported window and uncertainty; do not turn those two facts alone into certainty that marriage will occur.
