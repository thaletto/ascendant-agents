# Jaimini core

Use this reference when the topic rubric selects a Jaimini factor.

**Chara Karakas** are seven saved planet roles. A Karaka selects a topic role,
not a literal event or another person's private state. Atmakaraka is the self
and core-direction role; Darakaraka is the partnership role.

**Rashi Drishti** is sign-to-sign influence; use the saved sign-aspect map,
not Parashari planetary aspects or degree orbs. **Karakamsha** is the D9 sign
occupied by Atmakaraka, not a replacement natal chart.

**Arudha Padas** describe projected expression. **Upapada** is the twelfth-
house Pada for partnership themes. **Argala** records support and obstruction;
read its contributors and blockers as evidence, not a score.

Judge co-primary Parashari and Jaimini factors by their own rules. Agreement
strengthens natal confidence; equal-rank disagreement remains mixed.
