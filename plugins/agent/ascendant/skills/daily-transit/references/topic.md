# Daily transit hierarchy

Required saved evidence: D1, provenance, and dated transit positions. Dasha,
yogas, and SAV are optional only when the user's question explicitly needs
their context.

| Rule | Factor | Layer | Rank | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `PR-DAI-NATAL` | Natal house and lord relevant to the user's question | Natal promise | Primary | Applicable `BPHS-11.*`, `BPHS-11.14-16` |
| `PR-DAI-TRANSIT` | Dated planets activating that natal house or lord | Transit trigger | Primary | Ascendant methodology |
| `PR-DAI-DASHA` | Active MD/AD when the question concerns a longer timing window | Vimshottari activation | Modifying | `BPHS-46.3,14`; Ascendant methodology |
| `PR-DAI-SAV` | SAV for the activated natal sign when useful | SAV corroboration | Background | `BPHS-72.1-7`; Ascendant methodology |

State the resolved timestamp and timezone. Describe the transit as a temporary
activation of natal context, not a guaranteed mood or event.

## Jaimini natal context

| Rule | Factor | Rank | Authority |

| `JM-DAI-NATAL` | The topic-relevant Chara Karaka, Karakamsha sign, or Arudha Pada named by the matching topic rubric | Primary | Applicable `JS-1.1`, `JS-1.2`; `AJV3` |

Compare this natal context with the Parashari natal factor. Apply transits only
through the existing transit layer; this method defines no Jaimini transit or
